# Why you can trust Chit & Bridge

*A plain-language trust base — written for a customer, not an engineer. Companion to `KB-your-data-is-safe.md`.*

## You don't read the code — you trust the answers
Nobody who uses Gmail or WhatsApp has seen the code inside. You trust them because, over years, the hard
questions — *Can someone else read mine? What if it loses my data? What if it gets hacked?* — got answered
well enough that you stopped asking.

Chit & Bridge is new, so we won't ask you to trust a reputation we haven't earned yet. Instead we answer those
same hard questions up front, in plain language, and show how. **Judge us on the answers.**

## What we protect — in everyday terms

**1. Your records are yours alone.** Another business on the platform can never see your chits, orders, prices,
or history — and you can't see theirs. This isn't a checkbox we might forget; it's built into the foundation,
the way a bank can't show your statement to another customer. The only thing ever shared is what *you*
deliberately send to someone.

**2. Both sides hold a copy — like a signed receipt.** Every deal lands as two copies, one for each party, on
the same thread. Neither side can quietly change the other's copy. It's the digital version of a carbon-copy
receipt both people sign and keep.

**3. Everything is written down — who, what, when.** Every action — created, accepted, disputed, completed — is
recorded in a complete, time-stamped history, like a courier's tracking log. If there's ever a question about
"what happened," the record answers it.

**4. Disagreements have a place to go.** If something's wrong with a deal, either side can raise a dispute
inside the system. It's recorded, both sides see it, and it's resolved on the record — not in a lost email thread.

**5. It's locked in transit, and we check who you are.** Everything travels encrypted (the padlock, like online
banking). We verify your identity from a signed token on every request — we never simply take your word for it.

**6. Actions finish fully, or cleanly stop.** When you send or assign something that touches several records at
once, it either completes entirely or rolls back to where it was — never left half-done. The same principle a
bank uses so money never vanishes mid-transfer.

**7. If something breaks, it's caught — and traceable.** The system watches itself: errors are caught, you get a
clear message instead of a confusing crash, and every request carries a tracking id so a problem can be found
and fixed quickly. It fails safe, not silent.

## The hard questions we've already answered
| The worry | Our answer |
|---|---|
| "Can another business see my data?" | No — isolation is built in; your identity is proven, never assumed. |
| "Could someone guess their way to my records?" | No — asking for a record you don't own returns *"not found."* |
| "Could a malicious entry attack my screen?" | No — everything shown is sanitised; injected content can't run. |
| "What if it half-finishes an action?" | It completes fully or rolls back — never partial. |
| "What about thousands of login attempts?" | Rate-limited and blocked. |
| "Will an internal error leak details to an attacker?" | No — details stay in our logs; you get a plain message. |
| "Who can change my deal?" | Only the rightful party; every change is recorded with their name and time. |

Every one of these is checked against a written engineering checklist **before any change ships** — the same
discipline that, over time, turns a new product into a trusted one.

## Our half of the trust, and yours
- **We provide:** privacy isolation, encryption, an honest time-stamped record, safe failure, and disciplined
  engineering.
- **You keep:** your login private, use the official app, and tell us if anything looks off. Your login is the
  one key to your data — guard it like your bank PIN.

## Honestly, where we are
We're early. We'd rather earn your trust by showing you the engineering than by claiming a scale we don't have
yet. The foundations — privacy, an honest record, safe failure — are built in from day one, not bolted on
later. That is the part that's hard to add afterward, and it's the part we got right first.
