**(b) Conflicts with rules and constructs**
- "Country + currency FIXED, NO conversion path" (area pack) is about the FUNCTIONAL currency; a foreign bill keeps its own currency and the books stay in one functional currency — no conflict, said so in the PR.
- One calculation: the gain/loss and the revaluation are computed ONLY in the engine (fx_settlement, fx_revalue, and a pure fxRevaluation() builder the to-do and the route both call); screens paint.
- Finance suggests, owner decides: revaluation is proposed on the to-do and posted by the OWNER's press (and its reversal dated the next period's first day in the same transaction) — never automatically at lock.
- A rate is evidence: rates are owner-entered or imported (CSV) rows, insert-only; no third-party fetch; the entry names source + date.
