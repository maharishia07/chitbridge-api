## Why
1. **The bill travels without its tax.** `chitOf()` (api `tools/tally-connector/till.html:5918`) sends per line only
   `price · quantity · total (= net) · gst_rate · hsn · offer`. It drops the bill's `taxable`, `tax`, `by_rate`, `heads`,
   `supply`, the TOTAL (taxable + tax, rounded once — ₹1,118.16) and each line's `gross · taxable · tax`.
   → the server's `total_value` is the sum of line nets (₹1,118.15): **the 1-paisa gap is the invoice's round-off, lost**.
   → anything rebuilt from the copy (the reprint, the buyer's popup) finds no tax and no gross.
2. **The server works the tax out again, its own way.** `lib/tax-copy` recomputes from the lines and decides the supply
   from the two parties' states → **IGST**; the counter decided **intra** (CGST + SGST). The counter's
   `CBTax.supplyType(sc, sc)` (`till.html:17455`) compares the SHOP's state with ITSELF — the buyer's state is never
   consulted, so a counter bill is always intra.
3. **A duplicate invoice prints a different document.** `reprintOld` (`till.html:23701`) feeds `showSlip` the server
   row; its comment promises "the same layout, so a customer sees the same bill" — the layout is the same, the figures
   are not (GST ₹0.00 on a TAX INVOICE).

## What "as per the law" needs (CGST Act s.31 + Rule 46; the buyer's ITC s.16 rests on the invoice AS ISSUED)
- ONE invoice: the figures printed for the customer are the figures in BOTH ledgers and in every reprint/duplicate.
  Neither the server nor the buyer's screen may recompute a different tax for an issued invoice.
- The tax heads follow the PLACE OF SUPPLY. ⚠️ **A ruling for the CA, not for code:** an over-the-counter sale to a
  registered buyer from another state — CGST+SGST (supply where the goods are handed over) or IGST? Today the counter
  says one and the server the other. Whichever the CA rules, it is ONE rule, applied once.
- Round-off is declared, not lost (the invoice's total = taxable + tax, rounded once, with the round-off shown).

## Fix, in order (proposal — nothing changed yet)
1. **The bill carries its invoice** — `chitOf` adds the computed `taxable · tax · total · round_off · supply · by_rate ·
   heads` and per line `gross · taxable · tax · cgst · sgst · igst`. The server STORES them as issued.
2. **The server posts what was issued** — tax-copy uses the carried figures for a counter bill; it may recompute only
   to CHECK, and a difference is raised (named, never silently replaced). The seller's and buyer's postings read the
   same carried figures.
3. **Place of supply — one rule**, from the CA's ruling, in ONE function both the counter and the server call
   (`CBTax.supplyType(shopState, placeOfSupplyState)`), the buyer's state passed in for a B2B bill.
4. **Reprint and buyer's popup render the carried invoice** — the same slip, the same numbers.
5. **Bills already sent** (0007, 0008, 0009 and earlier test bills) — their copies lack the figures; the counter still
   holds them locally. A one-off re-send/back-fill of the invoice block, or leave test data as it is — Athi's call.
Proof: a bill rung → printed slip · server copy · seller ledger · buyer ledger · reprint · buyer popup — six readings,
one set of figures, to the paisa; a break that drops any carried figure must turn it red.
