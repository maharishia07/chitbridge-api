# Seat matrix: who may do what with the shop's money

GENERATED by `node tools/seat-matrix.cjs --write` from the route gates (routes/books.js), the books contract (lib/books-contract.js) and the access predicates (lib/owner.js, lib/access.js). Never hand-edited; the guard `tests/seat-matrix.test.cjs` fails when it drifts.

Seats: **Owner** · **Accountant** = a staff login whose hat is manager and who can edit (RC-7, the shop's own accountant or bookkeeper) · **Counter** = a staff login with hat act at editor level (the till seat; any plain editor) · **Viewer** = read-only staff · **Adviser** / **Auditor** = a firm under a live books grant (reads only unless a door is named). `yes` may · `-` refused (403) · `money in` the seat may record money in, not pay a supplier. The hat gives the access; choosing the Bookkeeper or Accountant label in Add staff sets the manager hat (DECISIONS 22 Nov, item 5).
